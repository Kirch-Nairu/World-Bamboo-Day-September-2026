# Open Decisions for Discovery

## Governance
- Who is the program owner?
- Who is the contracting / paying client?
- Who owns participant data?
- Who owns the final source code and production accounts?

## Payment
- Which organization legally receives participant payments?
- What does the payment represent: registration, seedling purchase, sponsorship, package, donation, or another category?
- Can an existing DTI/LGU/institutional payment facility be reused?
- Who approves refunds and reconciles collections?

## Event operations
- Exact site in Loboc.
- Maximum physical capacity.
- Planting-unit mechanics and inventory.
- Participant categories and group registration rules.
- Venue connectivity and event-day devices.

## Infrastructure
- Can DTI/LGU provide a subdomain?
- Can DTI/DICT provide hosting, database, email, backups or monitoring?
- Is external cloud/VPS hosting approved if institutional provisioning is too slow?

## Budget
- Approved / expected budget range.
- Whether infrastructure and transaction fees are inside or outside that budget.
- Whether event-day technical support is remote or on-site.
